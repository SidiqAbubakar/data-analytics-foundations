DATA ANALYTICS FOUNDATIONS - DAYS 1 TO 3 DATASET

Day 1: raw_sales.csv
Use this file in Excel. It intentionally contains data-quality problems. Identify and justify your cleaning decisions. Do not assume every row is correct.

Days 2-3: PostgreSQL
Import customers.csv, products.csv and orders.csv into PostgreSQL. Design appropriate data types, primary keys and foreign keys. Use SQL to answer the questions in your training guide.

Business context:
You are a junior data analyst for a Ghana-based retail company selling technology and office products. Management wants to understand revenue, customers, products, regions, salespeople and trends.

Important:
- No answer sheet is provided.
- Revenue is not stored as a field; calculate it from quantity and unit price where appropriate.
- Some patterns are intentionally present in the data. Discover them through analysis.
- Keep raw data unchanged and perform cleaning in a copy/workbook or through documented transformations.
