Findings	
1. Greater Accra and Ashanti region generated the most revenue
2. Northern and Volta region performed poorly in revenue generation
3. Performance laptop emerged the most performing product, with 37% of total top 5 performing products
4. Office Laptop follows performance laptop as the second most performing product contributing 26% of the total share
5. The salespersons generally did well with Esther been the only person to cross 700,000.00 GHS
	
	
RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Much attention needs to be given to Northern and volta in other to help acquire more sales from those regions
2.  There should be more advertisement for office Laptop and mini pc to help generate more income. They are the second and third products with most generated revenue
3. The more quantity sold the more salesperson generate. Hence incentivize quantity sold to encourage the sales team to sell more.
